# FIRE Investing Masterclass — Consolidated Mental Model

Synthesized from all 18 transcripts (Phase 2). This is the methodology corpus that
grounds the experts in Phase 4. Numbers/thresholds are quoted as taught — they are
the instructor's rules, not financial advice, and some are simplified for teaching.

**Instructor & brand:** "Eric / Mr. Fired Up Wealth" — a self-described *finance
coach (not a financial advisor)*, ~25 yrs investing, semi-retired early 40s, ex-Oracle
software sales, ~$1.7M (taught) / $1.6M taxable brokerage. Community: Patreon / Elite /
VIP, Discord (with a criteria "bot"), weekly "Chart Day" (Wed 9am ET). Signature
philosophy: **FIRE-D = Financial Independence, Retire Early *with Dividends*** — build
wealth with growth while young, rotate into dividends to live off passive income
*without ever selling principal* (explicitly rejects the 4% rule).

## 1. The portfolio system (the spine of the course)

A **5-bucket portfolio** scored by a **1–10 risk dial**. Buckets: **ETFs / DGF /
Growth / Spec / Income** (+ Cash). Risk-tiered blueprints:

| Blueprint | ETF | DGF | Growth | Spec | Income/Bonds | Cash |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **8/10 aggressive** (flagship, young) | 25% | 20% | 40% | 10% | — | 5% |
| **6/10 aggressive** | 35% | 30% | 20% | — | 10% bonds/other | 5% |
| **4/10 early retirement** | 50% (incl. ~25% VYM) | 35% DGI/DGF/income blend | — | — | 15% bonds/cash/other (real estate) | — |
| **$5M end-game (FIRE)** | 40% ($2M) | 30% dividends ($1.5M) | 25% ($1.25M) | — | (in dividends) | 5% ($250K, +bonds) |

**Per-bucket sizing rules (8/10):** DGF 10–25 names @ 1–2% each; Growth 10–25 names @
2–4% each; Spec 10–20 names @ ~$500–$1,000 ("10x or zero"); Cash in a liquid sweep.
**Hard concentration cap: ≤5% in any one stock** (often 2–3%).

**$5M end-game detail:** 40% ETF (SCHD $1.75M @3.1% + VOO $250K @1.45% → 2.9% → $58K/yr);
30% dividends (50 stocks × $30K, blended 4.2% → $63K/yr, mix monthly+quarterly payers,
incl. preferreds); 25% growth ($1M mature + $250K hyper, run covered calls on it);
5% cash/bonds. Total ≈ **$121K/yr (~2.5% yield) = ~$10,083/mo**, never reinvested,
grows to outpace inflation. Transformation from 8/10: ETF↔Growth flip-flop, DGF+Spec
merge into one dividend pool, spec disappears.

**Lifecycle ("metamorphosis"):** a stock and the whole portfolio migrate **Spec →
Hyper-growth → Growth → Mature growth → DGF → DGI → Income** over years; the risk dial
falls as you approach the income end-game.

## 2. The dividend buckets (highest-value reusable rules)

**DGF — "Dividend Growth for FIRE" (5 criteria, goal = total return):**
1. Dividend yield **≤ 3%** (best are ~1–1.5%)
2. Payout ratio **≤ 50%** (prefer **≤ 35%**)
3. **≥ 5 years** of dividend growth
4. **≥ 10%** dividend growth over that 5-yr period
5. **≥ 10%** 10-yr annualized (price/total) growth

**DGI — "Dividend Growth Investing":** focus on *growth of the dividend* (≥ inflation),
paid from current profits (not debt/accumulated capital); diversify industries; DRIP.
**Income stock:** owned for yield (bond-like), high payout, doesn't fit DGF/DGI — rule:
**yield > 4% + high payout, or any REIT**. **Avoid chasing yield: ≥ 6% is off-limits.**
"Higher yield = higher risk." DGF examples: AAPL, V, MSFT, UNH, MCD, COST, HON, ROP, DE.

## 3. Stock selection & evaluation (fundamentals)

**Coined method "Fundatech" / Fired Up Wealth:** *fundamentals are primary ("the movie
script of the future"); technicals only refine entry/exit.* Two analysis types:
**quantitative** (the numbers) + **qualitative** (management, moat, TAM, story — "where
humans beat AI"). Every metric is "a tool in the toolbox" — never decide on one.

**10 Due-Diligence steps (MC4):** 1) market cap; 2) revenue/profit/margin trends (+ P/S =
mktcap÷sales, P/E = price÷EPS, Rule of 40 for SaaS); 3) moat/competition; 4) bucket +
PEG (=P/E÷growth, <1 good) + P/B; 5) management & insider ownership; 6) balance sheet;
7) price history (+ RSI/MACD/MAs for entry); 8) options/dilution; 9) qualitative deep
dive (estimates, patents, TAM); 10) risks (macro, industry, legal, ESG).

**Financial statements (MC4, 7, 9):**
- **Balance sheet** (Assets = Liabilities + Equity): cash vs total assets YoY, AR vs
  sales, inventory, goodwill, **current ratio = CA/CL (want ≥ 1, prefer higher)**,
  **quick ratio = (CA−inv)/CL**, working capital, **net tangible assets = equity −
  goodwill**. Weak signals: negative retained earnings, negative equity, negative net
  tangible assets, low current ratio. "Sweet spot" = improving balance sheet + rising
  revenue + rising margins together.
- **Income statement** (Revenue − Expenses = Profit): gross margin = GP/rev (NVDA ex.
  62.3%), net margin = NI/rev (~26%), operating income (EBIT) vs EBITDA, EPS basic vs
  diluted. Earnings can be smoothed — distrust vs cash.
- **Cash flow** (OCF / CFI / CFF): operating cash flow is "renewable" and hardest to
  fake; **FCF = OCF − replacement CapEx** (don't subtract growth CapEx).
- **Red-flag thresholds (MC9):** debt-to-equity **> 100%** = red flag; **interest-coverage
  concern** — taught as *net interest ÷ operating earnings < 5* (⚠ this is inverted vs the
  standard interest-coverage ratio and is likely misspoken; quote-as-taught, **verify
  before encoding in an expert**); current ratio **< 1.5** / quick **< 1** = concern (but
  see addendum: <1 isn't always bad); **≥ 3 yrs** declining revenue = avoid; rising share
  count (dilution); payout **> 90%** (REITs exempt; DGF ideal ~20%, ≤50% acceptable).

**Peter Lynch (MC5) — 6 categories:** slow growers (income, 3–15%), stalwarts (blue
chips, 10–12% EPS growth, buy for 30–50%), **fast growers (favorite, 20–25%, seek
10-baggers)**, cyclicals, asset plays, turnarounds. Key tenets: limited downside /
unlimited upside; **be right 6 of 10**; "time in the market > timing"; buy what you
understand; ≥3 reasons to buy or don't.

**Megatrends (where to hunt):** AI, data centers, cloud, cybersecurity, aerospace/
defense, space, gaming, AR/VR, autonomous/EV, genomics, robotics, 5G+, e-commerce,
crypto/metaverse, clean energy. **"Semiconductors are the new oil"** (pick-and-shovel).

## 4. Technical analysis (entry/exit only)

**3 indicator buckets:** Price (trend), Volume (sentiment), Oscillators. Core tools:
**moving averages, trend lines, RSI** (MC6) + **candlesticks & chart patterns** (MC10).
- **MAs:** SMA vs EMA (EMA = less lag, growth stocks "follow the EMA until they don't").
  Long-term investors use **50/100/200-day**; **200-day = most important support**
  ("self-fulfilling" via algos). High conviction → buy at 50/100; break of 200 → "load up";
  expect a retest. Worked ex: Dollar General bought ~$194 near 200-day ~$195.
- **RSI** (Wilder, 0–100): **>70 overbought, <30 oversold**; ~90+ → watch for ~20%
  correction / trim. Use WITH MAs (MAs = trend, RSI = overbought/oversold).
- **Candlesticks (MC10):** anatomy (body/wick, green=bullish/red=bearish); single
  (hammer family, doji family, marubozu, spinning tops), double (engulfing, harami,
  tweezers), triple (morning/evening star, three white soldiers / black crows). His
  highest-win: **bull flags & pennants**; bullish engulfing/morning doji star = buy;
  bearish engulfing/dark cloud/shooting star/hanging man = sell.
- **Chart patterns:** reversal (double top/bottom, H&S, wedges), continuation (flags,
  pennants, rectangles), bilateral (triangles — *identical shapes can break either way;
  read the candles at the apex*; target Fibonacci levels). "One thing alone is never a
  guarantee" — combine candles + patterns + RSI/MACD/MAs + trend lines.

## 5. Buy methodology (MC3, 15)

Sequence: **financial education → vision/megatrends → blueprint (suitability = willingness
AND ability) → qualitative story → quantitative valuation (3–4 sources, never one metric)
→ technical entry (the "last 1%").** New investors: buy an ETF core (VOO) and DCA while
learning; avoid spec until ready.

**DCA (two modes):** (1) **periodic** — fixed $ every paycheck into core ETFs;
(2) **"FUWTALAS" lot-allocation** — buy a target position in lots at technical levels:
**20–25% per lot** (bull market 2–3 lots; bear market 10–30 lots). Never buy a full
position at once. DRIP introduces **yield-on-cost**.

**Valuation metrics:** P/E (trailing vs forward), PEG (≤1 attractive; 0.5 = investigate),
P/S (when unprofitable), P/B (<1 = margin of safety), plus EV/NTM-rev (SaaS), EV/EBITDA,
FCF yield, DCF, DDM, sum-of-parts. Cross-check ≥3 sources (Y Charts vs Fidelity vs your
own math). Qualified-dividend tax tiers: **0/15/18.8/23.8%** (+3.8% NII surtax).

## 6. Sell methodology (MC14) — price-action-agnostic

**Never sell on price alone.** Ask: why did you buy it, does it still fit the blueprint,
is the *business* broken or the *stock* broken? **12 sell reasons:** 1) no longer fits
blueprint; 2) story changed (growth/management/moat/margins/debt deterioration); 3)
acquisition (cash deal → lock gains near offer; stock deal → do you want the acquirer);
4) you genuinely need the money (avoid); 5) rebalancing (trim back toward ≤5%); 6) greener
grass (higher-conviction redeploy); 7) happy/overvalued (trim parabolic); 8) cash-covered
call exit; 9) story-driven loss-cut (don't average down to fix cost basis); 10) cut losses
& reposition; 11) tax-loss harvesting; 12) don't sell on FUD.

**Tax rules:** up to **$3,000** net loss offsets ordinary income/yr (excess carries
forward); unlimited vs capital gains. **Wash sale:** can't claim loss if you rebuy
substantially identical within **30 days before/after (61-day window)**; SPY↔VOO count
as identical; DCA can trip it. **Loss-recovery math:** −50% needs +100%; −95% needs
**+1,900%** — deep losses are near-unrecoverable. Mantra: *"selling winners and holding
losers is cutting the flowers and watering the weeds."*

## 7. Options for income (MC11)

1 contract = 100 shares; buyers have rights, sellers have obligations; options decay daily.
- **Cash-covered call** (his #1, "how the rich get richer"): own ≥100 shares, sell a call,
  collect premium; risk = opportunity cost (capped upside). Can **roll** out/up.
- **Cash-secured put** (get paid to buy lower): sell a put, reserve the cash; effective
  buy price = strike − premium (Buffett/Coke 1993: $35 strike, $1.50 premium → $33.50).
  Only cash-secured, **never naked/margin**.
- **Hedging / married put:** buy a put as insurance (max loss = premium); ≠ short selling.

## 8. Psychology & discipline (MC8) — "the most important piece"

**FOMO** (fear of missing out) vs **FOLE** (fear of losing everything). **Market-emotion
cycle:** optimism → euphoria (**max financial risk**) → … → capitulation → depression
(**max financial opportunity**) → optimism. **7 biases:** mental accounting, herd
behavior, emotional gap, anchoring, self-attribution, overconfidence, regret avoidance.
**Loss aversion:** losses felt **~2× more** than equivalent gains (Kahneman–Tversky).
**Barber & O'Dean:** 78k households — least-active 18.5%/yr vs most-active 11.4% (−7.1%).
**MPT** (Markowitz 1952): diversify; ~300-stock ideal vs avg investor's 3–4.

**Top 15 Investing Rules (recurring spine):** 1) only invest what you can afford to lose;
2) don't buy all at once (DCA 20–25%/lot); 3) no FOMO/FOLE; 4) take profits on parabolic;
5) diversify; 6) be robotic / no coulda-shoulda-woulda; 7) ≥3 reasons or don't buy;
8) patience; 9) embrace corrections; 10) keep **5–10% cash** (dry powder); 11) cut losses;
12) beware hype; 13) focus on moats/best-in-breed; 14) make your own path; 15) **have a
plan and stick to it** (most important).

## 9. Personal finance foundation (MC12)

3 keys: financial plan → pay off high-interest debt → save & invest. **Credit cards:** pay
in full monthly, prioritize all debt by interest rate (APR 18%+ beats any investment).
**Account-priority (his order):** 401k to match → Roth IRA (backdoor if needed) → ESPP
(if 15% discount) → taxable brokerage (bridge to 59½). Standard order adds Traditional
IRA, max 401k ($19,500 ex.), 529, **HSA** ("a Roth for medical"). **Goals:** write them
vividly, **SMART** (Specific/Measurable/Attainable/Realistic/Time-bound), life goals →
financial plan → blueprint *last*; review monthly/quarterly with spouse. Compounding =
"the eighth wonder"; "it's not how much you make, it's how much you save."

**DD process & governance (MC4):** DD is **two-phase** — *Phase 1 idea generation*
(portfolio-gap analysis → macro/secular trend → "use what you know" → best-of-breed-few-own)
then *Phase 2 the 10 steps*. Governance rules: **devil's advocate / stay neutral** (don't

qualitative review-source checklist (Google/Yelp/BBB/Consumer Reports + ask people).

**Balance sheet (MC4/7):** cash-jump diagnostic (good if from operations, bad if from
debt/asset-sales/dilution; idle cash also bad); AR↑ or inventory↑ vs sales = collection/
demand red flag; **3 strong-balance-sheet signals** = cash+short-term investments / low-zero
LT debt / undervalued assets; working capital = banker's loan test; "balance sheet is
bread-and-butter, hardest to manipulate"; current ratio <1 not always bad (Coke/Walmart),
varies by industry. One concrete screen: high current ratio + high cash + low debt.

**Income statement (MC9):** **gross & net margin consistently above peers = moat signal**;
**R&D scale = moat / low-R&D-dependence = lower obsolescence risk** (Kodak/Blockbuster);
SG&A only meaningful vs revenue (low = brand strength); **revenue quality** (recurring/SaaS
reliable; AR-inflated not); EBITDA explicitly criticized ("depreciation is a real expense");
**ROE = NI ÷ equity**; value-trap = low trailing P/E that worsens.

**Cash flow (MC9):** renewability hierarchy **OCF > CFI > CFF**; neg-OCF + pos-CFI =
liquidating to survive (bad); CFF positive for growth cos / negative for mature cos;
anomaly-investigation method (Wingstop special-dividend dig).

**Lynch (MC5):** **innings framework** (buy in the 2nd–3rd inning, not the 1st — SaaS 2019
IPOs); **diworsification** red flag (M&A outside core competency); category bands —
slow 3–15% (7–10% yield), stalwarts 10–12% EPS (ride 30–50%), fast 20–25% (>50% needs a
moat), turnarounds = "no growers"; "30 best days" stat ($341,722 vs $153,792); the
influencer pantheon (Graham, Bogle, Kiyosaki, Wood, Najarians…).

**Technical (MC6/10):** read RSI/MAs on the **1-day** chart (RSI is timeframe-dependent:
CF 92/73/68 on 1mo/1wk/1day); **20-day SMA** = short-term support (Bollinger); **SMA for
support/resistance, EMA for growth-stock trend**; **volume amplifies candle signals**;
Fibonacci breakdown ≈ erase **~2/3** of the move; **pattern-vs-candle precedence** — a
pattern isn't directional alone; read the candles at the apex (generalizes to all patterns).

**DCA & buy (MC3/15/18):** **"core" = ETFs + DGF + highest-conviction growth at 3–5%**;
mode→bucket mapping (**periodic → core/ETFs; FUWTALAS lot-allocation → fringe/individual**);
lots = **20–25% each** (bull 2–3 lots / bear 10–30 lots); **DRIP is itself a micro-DCA** →
yield-on-cost; accumulation phase = buy constantly, preservation (~$1M+) = selective;
**house shares** (sell initial cost basis, let the rest ride); goal pipeline = life goals →
plan → blueprint **last**, target via calculator, **return assumption ≤12% (8–9% conservative)**.
